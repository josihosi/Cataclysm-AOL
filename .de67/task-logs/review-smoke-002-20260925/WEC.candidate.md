## 1. Bandit dispatch gets stuck returning

**Observed problem.** The bandit structural dispatch from `(127,137,0)` has two surviving members, IDs 4 and 5, in `returning_home`. It remains at waypoint 3 of a five-OMT route, with current route tile `(126,135,0)` about two OMTs from camp. Repeated [log entries](.userdata/windows-camp-fixed-20260924/config/debug.log#L586254) show zero homeward steps or crossings and no paired loaded entry or staging positions. `missing_deadline_elapsed` means its deadline has already passed.

**Observed working.** The outing was created, both member identities survive, and its homeward route and state are retained. The problem is that travel stops near camp.

**Code clue.** [Loaded entry search](src/do_turn.cpp#L6378) requires two adjacent valid squares on the exact route OMT, which is outside the current loaded map. The [route-edge fallback](src/do_turn.cpp#L6492) is unavailable for this outing (`abstract_resume=no`, `cohesion_assembled=no`, `recenter_gate=no`), and [materialization is skipped](src/do_turn.cpp#L6695). This explains the repeated failure; it does not make abstract travel a completed return.

**Change wanted.** Keep the same surviving pair moving home abstractly while its route OMT is outside the loaded bubble. Materialize the same people at a valid loaded entry when appropriate, and resume abstract travel if the bubble moves away. Mark the return complete only after genuine arrival at camp.

**Targeted playtest.** Start from a copy of the [camp-modified open-window scenario](.userdata/windows-camp-20260924/save/TestSetup00/) for a natural dispatch and return; also use a copy of the [observed checkpoint](.userdata/windows-camp-fixed-20260924/save/TestSetup00/) to exercise the already-stuck pair. Move the bubble across the home route and wait through return. Check continuing waypoints, pair IDs, loaded/abstract handoff, casualties, and actual camp arrival. A placement failure must leave a truthful active journey, not a frozen cursor or false success.

The downstream contact dispatcher can accept smoke, but a smoke-only first packet does not reach it through this path ([callback owner](src/do_turn.cpp#L9065)).

**Change wanted.** A valid first smoke signal reaches the same camp response decision as first light. It can initiate needed NPC materialization and dispatch without prior light, under the same ownership and duplicate-prevention rules.

## 3. A living camp is omitted as an observer

**Observed problem.** The ecology observer displayed the cannibal camp as active and populated, yet [staffed-camp observation](.userdata/windows-camp-fixed-20260924/config/debug.log#L99) repeatedly logged `sites=3 eligible=0 callbacks=0`. The counter stayed zero after a scout dispatch appeared.

**Code clue.** [Camp observer selection](src/bandit_live_world.cpp#L15505) requires a healthy concrete member marked `at_home`, so the abstract staffed camp is missed. It then skips the camp when an outing is active, despite a remaining home member. The [ecology display](src/ecology_debug_view.cpp#L494) uses the valid roster and living count, explaining the mismatch.

**Change wanted.** **Staffed camps and active dispatches are the complete observer set.** A valid living home roster makes a camp an observer whether abstract or loaded and whether or not a dispatch exists. **Targeted playtest.** From the [camp-modified open-window start](.userdata/windows-camp-20260924/save/TestSetup00/), check camp observation before any dispatch, while members are abstract, after materialization, and while a dispatch is away. ## 5. Hostile dispatches should search, fight, and leave

**Code clue.** The [loaded local gate](src/do_turn.cpp#L4813) handles exposure and player contact, while the [loaded raid target list](src/do_turn.cpp#L2616) focuses on the player and allies. These paths do not establish the requested full search or cannibal human-and-zombie target rule.

Search and departure progress continue abstractly when unloaded; a failed local move neither freezes nor falsely finishes the visit.
