# R-HARNESS-PERFORMANCE representative workload comparison

Metric interpretation: `game_turns_per_wall_second` and `game_minutes_per_wall_second` are retained window wall-span rates. They include inter-turn input, pause, transport and operator gaps, and are not simulation-throughput or regression evidence. Comparable advancing throughput is `game_turns_per_simulation_second` / `game_minutes_per_simulation_second`, whose denominator is the sum of completed native turn simulation seconds.

- `baseline.status=comparable`, retained reference and complete workload provenance.
