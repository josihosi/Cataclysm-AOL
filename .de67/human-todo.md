# Human todo

1. **Send a trade caravan out of a survivor settlement.** Start with one route, such as the commune to the Refugee Center. The player can meet the same traders on the road, buy something, and see the remaining cargo reach its destination. A radio call offering a rendezvous would give the player a first reason to decide whether to trust strangers.
2. **Find the worst pause in an ordinary long game.** Play a populated Housatonic save through a camp wait and a trip that brings moving scouts into and out of the loaded map. Keep the worst reproducible pause, fix its cause, and replay the same stretch. Check both how long the player waits and whether the scouts still finish their trip.
3. **Let a settlement scavenge its neighborhood.** Start with food at one nearby shop. If the player gets there first, the scavengers can take only what remains. If the scavengers get there first, the player can find the depleted shelves and see the haul at the settlement. That gives the caravan something local to trade.
4. **Tune how far overmap factions hear loud activity.** Start with existing gunfire and one proposed sustained source, a running diesel engine. Compare a quiet vehicle with the diesel on the same road. Check where a nearby camp investigates, where a distant one hears nothing, and what sustained noise costs in turn time.
