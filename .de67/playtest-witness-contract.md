# Live playtest witness contract

This contract applies only to active work marked `Playtest witness: required`. The coordinator
provides a compact charter with the outcome, material proof or contradiction, accepted evidence,
current uncertainty, forbidden credit, and evidence ceiling. It must not prescribe an interaction
sequence or turn ordinary fictional danger into a safety gate.

The brief and matching charter request execution. Querying remains inert and the registry supplies
single-use source/executable/scenario/run authority. The worker owns how to observe, act, repair,
change tactics, rerun under a valid binding, and finish. The immutable journal owns native facts,
receipts, contradictions, cleanup, and credit; a witness cannot alter them.

At finish, the worker states the smallest conclusion the cited journal supports and preserves full
responses, deterministic reads, screenshots, and material contradictions or unknowns as
digest-bound artifacts. Its compact result carries the exact bindings, stable journal identities,
useful narrow query keys, and first divergence without copying the full payload; complete evidence
remains explicitly retrievable when needed. A claim bundle gives independent verdicts to features
that shared the run and binds any routed defect to affected and explicitly unaffected claims; it
does not promote or suppress their evidence. Wording and action order are free. Mechanical validation
rejects absent or mismatched citations, identity/binding drift, concealed supplied contradictions,
unowned cleanup, invalid defect scope, and evidence promotion. The coordinator judges causal
sufficiency, records ordinary defects, and repairs derivable clerical data without replaying gameplay.
