## Shared start and proof

Prefer the rural dual-camp fixture for simultaneous cannibal/bandit logging, once native loading is validated. Keep the city variant for zombie pressure; it answers a different survival question. Both factions should progress naturally: do not synchronize dispatch, seed knowledge or force hostility to make the comparison tidy. Track each site's operation/generation, actual members and outcome separately. If one faction interferes with the other, retain that result and use a separate compatible single-faction arm only to isolate an unresolved mechanism.

Use ordinary doors/stairs and travel when testing that route; otherwise use permitted avatar teleportation to establish the required separation. Move far enough that the relevant actors actually unload, spend a full 24 game hours away for the day-away variants, and return. Time away starts at the declared departure boundary; record travel time separately. Do real looting when it is part of the assigned journey; use practical survival setup for continuity-only questions. Before departure, [get and verify avatar Debug Life Support](setup-and-interactions.md#get-and-verify-debug-life-support); keep it on for the day-away faction/handover arms. A separately named ordinary-needs survival comparison leaves it off with real supplies. Record setup consistently; no clairvoyance/cloak in ordinary exposure proof. Do not repair a failing handover by moving tested actors or editing missions/knowledge. The separately authorized [X4 casualty variant](playtest-suite.md#x4-party-survivor-and-casualty-handover) uses avatar teleportation/invisibility and one exact debug kill to create controlled boundary conditions; it does not claim ordinary travel or combat-death proof.

A short walk outside the house does not prove an unloaded handover. Native owner/cursor records and saved actor location/identity establish loading transitions; visibility alone does not. Capture relevant real IDs before crossing, including actual response parties rather than their scouts. Existing local NPC action trace does not cover abstract simulation: combine transition/owner records with retained actor saves and mark missing coverage honestly.

At meaningful crossings compare same-ID position/z, life/HP, mission/goal/path, operation phase, route cursor and simulation owner, watch progress, report revision, cargo/supply and payments when relevant. A pair must not duplicate, resurrect a casualty, lose a survivor, reset its journey or credit return/payment twice. Off-screen local combat need not be simulated at identical granularity; require continuity and evidence-supported knowledge, not invented distant fights. Save complete bytes at useful branching boundaries. Individual proven controls remain available while batch validation is pending.

## First pass: day-long absences at distinct phases

6a — Before discovery. Leave before lighting a new signal and stay away a day. Separate signal-off baseline from an already burning base; observe natural discovery rather than expecting it in both. Return and inspect fire, base state and faction knowledge. No unexplained live tracking of the absent player.

6b — Scouts outbound. After actual scout reservation/departure, leave their bubble for a day and return. Watch physical-to-abstract travel and rematerialization: same actors, route progress, no frozen departure or fresh duplicate scouts.

6c — Watch underway. Depart once actors truly occupy watch positions, not merely an abstract observing label. Return after a day. Watch must resolve according to actual observation/eligibility, retaining roof source height separately from ground watch position. An empty report is a valid result.

6d — Report homeward. Leave after watch ends while carriers are returning; return after a day. Preserve cargo/observations, same-ID physical arrival and exactly-once report. Check the pair arriving at camp as the bubble shifts; camp arrival must not be converted into another incomplete return.

6e — Response authorized/rallying. Leave after a real cannibal response or bandit expedition is reserved; return after a day. Inspect each faction's own timing, capability and destination. A night gate can be legitimate; do not copy cannibal timing to bandits. The base stays a known place, while an unseen avatar's new position is not automatic knowledge.

6f — Response approaching/local contact. Depart before contact in one branch; leave after genuine contact in a saved branch. Return a day later and establish real survivors, abandoned/completed operations, base damage/loot if implemented, and renewed contact. Defeated raiders are a valid outcome when combat evidence explains it. For the new bandit encounter contract, overmap-only destination time is pending local simulation, not refusal or invented combat; verify once-only activation. Cannibal raid semantics remain separate: establish actual existing behavior and flag a missing rule rather than assuming bandit waiting applies.

6g — Paid bandits departing. Pay through the real interaction, then leave a day while collectors head home. Verify retained payment/debt, cargo and departure/return, no duplicate charge or unjustified attack. Compare refusal/Fight only from a separate pre-choice branch.

## Exposure and pursuit branches

6h — Burn the watch, return home. Here “burn” means expose the covert scouts, not set them on fire. Approach through ordinary movement until the native reciprocal exposure/burn transition is actually recorded, then retreat to base. Observe the actual egress, alternate watch or withdrawal result, future report and response. Merely seeing a scout through debug vision does not prove their cover was blown.

6i — Burn, leave a day, return. From a separately retained exposed-watch boundary, leave both scouts' local area for 24 hours. Test withdrawal/alternate-watch ownership across unloading and reload. Do not expect the old unexposed watch to resume unchanged or invent an immediate raid.

6j — Seen escape versus unseen detour. After real hostile contact, break sight behind terrain, change direction and go home or to a separate loot site. Compare with departure that nobody sees. Pursuit must use actual sight/sound/last-known evidence; logging knows the avatar position but the faction must not inherit that omniscience. Record distinct faction pursuit and any natural cross-faction fight.

## Boundary stress branches, using the nearest compatible successful segment

6k — Bubble edge and split pair. Position the avatar, using permitted teleportation if useful, to load/unload a moving party, then cross back once, including a case with one scout inside and its partner outside. Inspect the first ownership transfer; repeat crossings only if they exercise a changed state. No duplicate actors, stranded partner, cursor rewind or boundary-corner stall.

6l — Survivor crossing. If combat naturally kills or incapacitates one member, cross the bubble and return; also retain a zero-survivor outcome when it occurs. Living members must retain their appropriate state, casualties must stay resolved. Natural death earns combat attribution only from native evidence. The owner-selected X4 debug kill is a supported separate setup variant for survivor/casualty persistence; it needs no repeated natural fight to manufacture the same condition.

6m — Save/reload at handover. Reuse a real pre-crossing or post-crossing checkpoint and reload once: outbound, exposed withdrawal or homeward as relevant to the question. Continue the same IDs; no lost pending crossing, repeated receipt, fresh party or restored dead actor. Prefer checkpoints from the above journeys over new ignition runs.

6n — Vertical and terrain boundary. Leave/return via actual house stairs and, in separate compatible terrain, bridge/ramp or underground connection. Vary avatar floor while scouts travel on ground. Evidence source z, physical route z and loaded actor z remain distinct; no through-floor movement. An unavailable connection is an honest terrain limit, not permission to alter the map midrun.

6o — Signal burnout and relight. Establish finite fuel and inspect automatic refueling sources
before leaving. Use the [fuel rules](../GAME-MANUAL.md#normalized-firewood-zones-and-stock) to
prevent unintended replenishment when burnout is the question; a maintained fire remaining lit
does not exercise burnout. Avatar teleportation can establish timely absence. Check actual loading
and elapsed time rather than assuming unloaded fire behaves identically to loaded fire. On return,
inspect both braziers before adding fuel: verify actual extinction/residual signals, then relight
through native controls and follow new observations without wiping remembered base knowledge.
If the first setup never burns out, adapt and complete that missing segment. This can extend a
compatible day-away journey; it does not need a duplicate campaign merely to record fire state.

## Why these boundaries

Current source has separate materialize/dematerialize structural pair owners, physical home-return receipts and persisted simulation cursors (`src/do_turn.cpp`: materialize_live_bandit_structural_handoffs, dematerialize_live_bandit_structural_handoffs, handoff_live_bandit_generic_scout_returns; `src/bandit_live_world.h`: local pair/casualty/cursor interfaces). Dematerialization explicitly preserves complete physical camp returns. Covert burn assessment uses reciprocal target/scout exposure and separate egress planning. These are different transitions and deserve different proofs; one successful stationary raid does not cover them.

Start with 6b/6c/6d, then 6h/6i and 6e/6f/6g. Extend with 6a and 6j–6o using already-earned compatible checkpoints. Run both factions in the shared world where feasible, without requiring both to reach the same phase simultaneously. Record subcase/build/result links under suite arm 6; later reruns include the adopted subcases. No new scheduler, registry or logging framework. If an expected transition has no usable native evidence, commission the smallest harness-only observation needed rather than guessing from silence.
