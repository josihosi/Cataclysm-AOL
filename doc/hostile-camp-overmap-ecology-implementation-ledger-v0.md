## Product contract

Naturally generated bandit and cannibal camps send exact two-person scouts through honest bounded
perception and physical movement. Scouts may watch quietly or be exposed, withdraw coherently,
return only evidence carried by real survivors, and cause one faction-specific decision. Bandits
produce shakedowns; cannibals produce night raids. Identity, ownership, persistence, performance,
save growth, and cross-platform behavior remain bounded and replay-safe.

Debug tooling observes or explicitly intervenes in authoritative owners. It never becomes player
knowledge, a second registry, or a substitute simulation.
