# Overmap AI field guide

Use this guide to follow a party from an observed signal to a mission outcome, or to find where a new species can join shared travel. The source baseline is C-AOL `84744b41e5c3`. Search the linked files for the named symbols. Current work on human movement also touches `do_turn.cpp` and its tests. Recheck their working diff before attributing a result to that commit.

## Why did this party stop?

1. **Identify the body, operation and observation.** In the ecology view, a Mi-go dispatch row carries a tower source ID, operation generation and member ID. Read its `omt`, `owner`, `loaded`, `state`, `signal_source` and optional `target`. For a human marker inspect `position_basis` before interpreting the coordinates. A checkpoint can differ from a member's current position. Keep the same identity through reload. [Ecology view](../src/ecology_debug_view.cpp)
2. **Find the last completed movement decision.** Follow the caller into `overmap_party_travel::prepare_transit`. Inspect `actor_input` (origin, destination, generation and lease epoch), the edge gate, paid work, and the returned `transit_status`. Waiting for edge work, retrying after refusal, and yielding to local movement have different next steps. If the tower has yet to dispatch a member, first trace the sample, receiver and response reservation below. For a dispatched Mi-go at a loaded boundary, start at `prepare_coarse_step`: on `local_handoff`, inspect the selected next tile and `local_waypoint`, then the local body owner and physical route. A coarse step alone cannot demonstrate a physical crossing. [Travel interface](../src/overmap_party_travel.h) · [Mi-go adapter](../src/migo_tower_native.cpp) · [Physical navigation](../src/physical_navigation.cpp)
3. **Locate the first missing observation.** A dispatch marker's target tells you where the party is headed. It does not expose every rejected receiver or local path decision. For signal intake, `receiver_observation` captures bounded diagnostics when supplied to `receive_signals`. For movement, inspect the adapter result and the same body's position before and after the step. If the only surviving evidence is a stationary marker, the cause stays open. [Receiver interface](../src/migo_tower_native.h) · [Debug projection](../src/ecology_debug_view.cpp)

## Human scout and Mi-go investigator

**Observation and mission.** For human scouts, follow `record_staffed_camp_signal_observations` or `record_structural_signal_observations` into camp knowledge. For a remembered lead, `plan_site_dispatch_from_camp_map_lead` checks the camp decision and selects eligible members. A Mi-go tower's `receive_signals` checks a real sample and eligible observer. Its record identifies the observer, source and turn. `begin_response` rechecks the signal, population and leases before reserving responders. A visible fire, a received signal and an authorized outing are three separately observable events. For smoke versus firelight, start with the shared time/weather/range vocabulary, then inspect the receiver's actual geometry and eligibility. [Camp decisions](../src/bandit_live_world.cpp) · [Visibility](../src/fire_signal_visibility.h) · [Tower reception and response](../src/migo_tower_native.cpp)

**Travel and crossing.** In the human scheduler, find the `prepare_transit( work, actors, policy )` calls. Follow the structural outing members, paid work and selected edges into physical admission under the pair's mission lease. In the Mi-go adapter, `prepare_coarse_step` binds the population identity and lease epoch, supplies the route finder, edge validator, terrain weight and phase gate, and passes them to the same travel preparation function. Local movement uses actual geometry. The actor adapter authenticates the body and commits its position. Compare the human test “structural shared route starts physical admission at the loaded boundary” with `migo_native_loop_boundary_handoff_yields_released_handle_and_preserves_refusal` before changing shared boundary behavior. [NPC scheduler](../src/do_turn.cpp) · [Shared implementation](../src/overmap_party_travel.cpp) · [Human controls](../tests/bandit_live_world_test.cpp) · [Mi-go controls](../tests/migo_tower_test.cpp)

**Disposition.** Human `apply_active_scout_observations` advances the outing. Follow `record_structural_member_physical_return` and `apply_return_packet` to distinguish walking home from communicating a report. For Mi-go, follow the pending sweep target through `service_operations`, including searched, hazardous or unreachable dispositions, then member return and reconciliation at the tower. The test `migo_actual_death_needs_physical_witness_then_home_communication` illustrates why a roster update alone cannot give the tower casualty knowledge. These are code and test references. Actual journey outcomes belong in the run evidence for the particular save and build. ## Adding a third species

Start by naming the observer, the evidence it may know, its mission decision and its canonical body owner. Implement the adapter that supplies `actor_input` and `transit_policy`, persists work for the same actor and operation, handles refusal or local handoff, and commits movement through that owner. Decide how a real local body completes the action and who receives its outcome. Supply a stable identity and an honest position source to the debug view. The shared interface provides routing and paid-edge preparation. Species registration, observation rules, mission state, persistence and local body admission still require concrete integration. [Interface](../src/overmap_party_travel.h) · [Work and retry controls](../tests/overmap_party_travel_test.cpp)

**Possible further sharing.** Both the human scheduler and Mi-go adapter assemble actor inputs and classify the next edge before committing movement. If a boundary change has to be made in both, compare the two gate decisions on the same loaded edge. A common geometric predicate may be useful. Human staged pair admission and tower population leases have different commit rules. Preserve both controls above, plus “cached NPC edge gate can veto a valid shared reroute”, before proposing any move of responsibility. This is an investigation candidate.
